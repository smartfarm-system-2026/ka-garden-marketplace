# 🌷 K & A Garden Marketplace

Welcome to **K & A Garden Marketplace**, a free local website where flower sellers and garden lovers can connect, discover plants, and share their blooms.

## 🌸 What You Can Do
- **Discover** flowers from local garden sellers
- **Buy** by contacting the seller directly
- **Sell** your own flowers, plants, and garden products for free

## 🌱 How to Sell Your Flower
1. Open the website and go to **Sell Your Flower**
2. Fill in your details: your real name, flower name, type, age, stocks, and location
3. Add your **contact number** (phone or Facebook number) so buyers can reach you
4. Choose a clear photo of your flower (JPG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 5 MB)
5. Click **Publish My Flower**

## 📌 Community Rules
Please keep the marketplace safe and friendly for everyone.
- Upload only photos of **flowers, plants, and garden products**
- **No nude, sexual, violent, or offensive photos.** Violators will be removed.
- Use your **real name** and **correct contact details**
- Sell honestly. Describe your flower as it really is.
